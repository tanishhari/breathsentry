    Q (fresh-air ventilation per person) is estimated from the CO₂ build-up in the room: Q = G / (C − Cout), with G = 0.005 L/s of CO₂ exhaled per person. Coughs and sneezes are classified from MFCC sound fingerprints — raw audio is never stored or transmitted.
